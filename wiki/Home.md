# Dark Heresy 2nd Edition: Expanded

Reference for the [Foundry VTT system](https://github.com/MattMavis/dark-heresy-2nd-vtt) — an
unofficial fork of [Matt Keathley's Dark Heresy 2nd Edition system](https://github.com/mrkeathley/dark-heresy-2nd-vtt),
extended with further automation.

This wiki is for people **playing with** the system. For installing it, the fork's additions, or
contributing, see the [README](https://github.com/MattMavis/dark-heresy-2nd-vtt#readme).

## Compendium reference

Every document in the system's 18 compendium packs, as browsable tables.

**→ [[Compendium]]**

These pages are generated from the packs themselves on every release, so they cannot drift out
of step with what the system actually ships. Do not edit them by hand — the next release will
overwrite your changes.

## How the automation works

What the system does for you, and the rulings it makes where the books are ambiguous.

- **[[Character Creation]]** — the eight-step wizard, what it grants, and what it cannot find
- **[[Experience and Advancement]]** — the XP ledger, Spend and Award, aptitude pricing, rank gating
- **[[Playing a Psyker]]** — Psy Rating, the Psyker elite advance, powers and their prerequisites
- **[[Requisition and Influence]]** — Availability modifiers, the combined test, Warband Subtlety

## A note on rulings

Where the books contradict themselves the system has to pick one reading, and those choices are
called out on the page they affect — see the Scarce availability note under
[[Requisition and Influence]] for an example. If a ruling is wrong for your table, the page says
which line of code to change.

Where a rule is genuinely a judgement call, the system's habit is to **advise rather than
enforce**: it shows you the number and the condition and leaves the decision to the GM. Talent
and psychic power prerequisites work this way, as do situational equipment bonuses.
