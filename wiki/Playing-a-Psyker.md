# Playing a Psyker

## Becoming one

Taking the **Psyker elite advance** grants the whole package rather than leaving you to assemble
it by hand:

- **Psy Rating 1**
- the **Psyker** trait
- the **Psyker** aptitude
- **1d10+3 Corruption**

Three rules the system enforces:

**A Sanctioned Psyker is not charged the Corruption.** Sanctioning is what the Scholastia
Psykana is for, and the character has paid for it elsewhere.

**The Corruption is rolled once.** A flag on the character records that the package was granted,
so re-applying the advance cannot re-roll Corruption onto them.

**An existing Psy Rating is never lowered.** A character who already has a rating keeps it.

## Powers and prerequisites

Psychic powers carry their prerequisites, and the Spend Experience list checks them against the
character the same way it does for talents. You can see at a glance what they qualify for
instead of looking each one up.

As with talents, a prerequisite the system cannot make sense of **never blocks a purchase** —
it falls back to advising and trusting the table.

### Wording the books print inconsistently

The same requirement is phrased several different ways across the line, and two are misspelled.
The system normalises these rather than failing to read them:

| As printed | Read as |
|---|---|
| `Rank 1 Psyniscience` | clause reordered to the talent form |
| `Str 40`, `Fellowship 35` | `S 40`, `Fel 35` |
| `5+ Insanity Points` | `Insanity 5` |
| `Endurace` | `Endurance` |
| `Spontanous` | `Spontaneous` |

If you add a homebrew power whose prerequisite will not parse, it simply stays advisory — there
is nothing to fix for the power to be buyable.

## What the compendium holds

76 powers, covering the Dark Heresy 2nd line only: the Core Rulebook, Enemies Within and Enemies
Beyond. See **[[Psychic-Powers]]** for the full table.

The pack previously carried 114, drawn from across the whole 40k RPG family — Rogue Trader, Into
the Storm, Tome of Fate, The Navis Primer and first-edition Dark Heresy — with nothing to tell
them apart from the DH2 ones. Those were removed in 1.8.6.5, along with ten unsourced
time-manipulation powers that appear in no published DH2 book.

**Powers already on a character were not touched** by that cull. Embedded items are copies, so a
power on a sheet stays there and works as before; only the browsable list is shorter. If your
table uses powers from the wider range, add them back by hand or keep them in a world compendium
of your own.
